# Game Vision
Life Paths: Rise or Ruin is a global life-simulation game where choices shape destiny.

## Gameplay Pillars
- Character creation
- Life simulation
- Events & dilemmas
- Destiny paths


## World Setting
A sprawling fantasy realm where kingdoms rise and fall, technology blends with magic, and every choice shifts the balance of power.

## Player Choices
- Career paths: warrior, scholar, merchant, mystic
- Relationships: alliances, rivalries, romance
- Morality: altruism vs ambition
- Survival: resource management, risk-taking

## Consequences
Choices ripple forward into:
- Character stats
- Branching storylines
- Global events (wars, prosperity, collapse)

## Endings
Multiple destinies:
- Rise to greatness
- Fall into ruin
- Wander in obscurity

## GTA-Style Inspiration
Life Paths: Rise or Ruin draws from GTA's immersive city feel but shifts focus:
- Jobs and careers instead of crime missions
- Languages and culture as unlockable opportunities
- Choices and consequences shaping destiny
- Multiple endings: greatness, ruin, or obscurity
